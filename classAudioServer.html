Detailed Description

Listens on the audio TCP port. When a client connects and completes the DSKFAUDIO handshake, it starts receiving Opus-encoded audio packets and playing them through the local audio output.

One AudioServer instance services all connected audio clients (mixing is first-writer-wins; only the first connected client is played at a time).
